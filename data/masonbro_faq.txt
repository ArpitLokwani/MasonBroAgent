# MASONBRO COMPANY INFORMATION

## Company Overview

MasonBro is a home masonry and construction services platform that connects customers with trusted masons for their home construction and renovation requirements.

Tagline: Your Trusted Mason, Just a Tap Away.

## Founders and Ownership

MasonBro is co-founded by:

1. Sushmi Lokwani – Founder & Product.
2. Arpit Lokwani – Co-Founder & Chief Technology Officer (CTO).

Ownership:
MasonBro is jointly owned by its two founders, with equal ownership of 50% each.

## Operating Location

MasonBro operates from Bangalore, Karnataka, India.

Currently, MasonBro provides home masonry and construction services to customers in Bangalore.

## Services

MasonBro currently offers the following services:

1. Brickwork and wall construction.
2. Plastering.
3. Flooring and tiling.

## Booking Process

1. Customers select the required service through the MasonBro platform.
2. Customers submit a booking request.
3. The MasonBro operations team assigns a suitable mason.
4. The assigned mason contacts the customer to understand the work requirements.
5. The mason may visit the property to inspect the work.
6. After inspection, the mason provides a quotation.
7. Work begins after the quotation is reviewed and approved.

## Customer Communication

The assigned mason may contact customers through phone calls or WhatsApp to:

- Understand the work requirements.
- Request photographs of the work area.
- Discuss project details.
- Coordinate property inspection.
- Share the quotation.

## Important Information

- MasonBro currently operates in Bangalore, India.
- A mason is assigned by the MasonBro operations team after a booking request.
- The quotation is provided after understanding and assessing the work.
- Work should begin only after the quotation has been reviewed and approved.
