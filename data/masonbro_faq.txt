
MASONBRO COMPANY INFORMATION

Company:
MasonBro provides home masonry and construction services.

Location:
MasonBro currently serves customers in Bangalore, India.

Services:
1. Brickwork and wall construction.
2. Plastering.
3. Flooring and tiling.

Booking Process:
1. Customers select a required service.
2. Customers submit a booking request.
3. The MasonBro operations team assigns a suitable mason.
4. The mason contacts the customer to understand the work.
5. The mason may visit the property for inspection.
6. The mason provides a quotation.
7. Work starts after the quotation is approved.

Customer Communication:
The assigned mason may contact customers through phone calls or WhatsApp to collect photographs and understand work requirements.

Important:
A quotation should be approved before the work begins.
